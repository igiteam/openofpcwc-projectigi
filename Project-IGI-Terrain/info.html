Overview

Project I.G.I.: I'm Going In is a single-player first-person shooter by Innerloop Studios, published by Eidos Interactive in 2000. Its campaign has 14 missions with three difficulty levels.

Story

The game opens somewhere in Russia, with Priboi questioning a prisoner who will not talk. From there the campaign follows Jones mission by mission:

  1. Trainyard: Small coastline trainyard
  2. SAM Base: Small missile defence base
  3. Military Airbase: Rescue Josef from the airbase
  4. GOD: Defend the friendly troops
  5. Radar Base: Disrupt Jach's communications network
  6. Get Priboi: Kidnap Jach from his base
  7. Border Crossing: Get Jones across the border
  8. Re-supply: Get Jones' equipment back
  9. Missile Trainyard: Get on the train
  10. Defend Priboi: Take Jach to the FRV
  11. Eagle's Nest I: The approach to Ekk's fortress
  12. Eagle's Nest II: Ekk's hilltop fortress
  13. Nuclear Infiltration: Infiltrate the nuclear base
  14. Finding the bomb: Kill Ekk and find the bomb

Characters

Jones
The player. Missions 7 and 8 are about getting him across the border and getting his equipment back.
Anya
In Mission 13 Jones secures a deployment zone for Anya and the friendly forces. She is with him at the end.
Josef Priboi
Held in the security building of the military airbase in Mission 3. The flight logs in the control tower give his new position.
Jach Priboi
His communications network is the target of Mission 5. Kidnapped from his base in Mission 6 and taken to the FRV in Mission 10.
Ekk
Her hilltop fortress is the Eagle's Nest of Missions 11 and 12. Mission 14: find Ekk, kill her, and find the bomb.
